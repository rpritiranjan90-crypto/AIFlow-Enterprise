# Customer Feedback Platform & Voice of Customer (VoC) Program

## Overview
AIFlow Enterprise collects real-time customer feedback through in-app widgets, Net Promoter Score (NPS) surveys, CSAT post-support ratings, and quarterly Customer Advisory Board (CAB) sessions.

---

## 1. Feedback Ingestion Channels
- **In-App Widget**: Direct feature requests & bug reports embedded in React UI.
- **NPS & CSAT Surveys**: Triggered after key user milestones (e.g., 5th workflow execution).
- **Voice of Customer (VoC) Interviews**: Monthly 30-minute structured feedback interviews with power users.
