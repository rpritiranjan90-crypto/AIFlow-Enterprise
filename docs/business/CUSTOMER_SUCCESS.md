# Customer Success & Support Plan

## 1. Onboarding Sequence (Email)
Triggered upon user registration in the `Free` or `Pro` tiers.
- **Day 0 (Welcome)**: Introduction to AIFlow Enterprise, linking to the Help Center.
- **Day 3 (Activation)**: "How to build your first AI agent" tutorial.
- **Day 7 (Engagement)**: "Connecting custom data sources" deep dive.
- **Day 14 (Conversion)**: "Scaling up: Why upgrade to Pro?"

## 2. Customer Health Scoring
Monitored via the Product Analytics dashboard.
- 🟢 **Green (Healthy)**: Logged in > 3 times this week, executed > 50 workflows.
- 🟡 **Yellow (At-Risk)**: No login in 14 days OR < 10 workflows executed. Trigger automated outreach.
- 🔴 **Red (Churn Warning)**: Payment failed OR 0 executions in 30 days.

## 3. Support SLAs
- **Enterprise**: 1-hour response time (24/7/365). Phone & Dedicated Slack channel.
- **Pro**: 12-hour response time (Business hours). Email only.
- **Free**: Community support only (No SLA).

## 4. Incident Communication Template
To be used for P1 Outages on the Status Page:
> **Subject**: [Investigating] Elevated Latency in Workflow Executions
> **Message**: We are currently investigating reports of delayed AI workflow executions. Our engineers are engaged and isolating the root cause. No data has been lost. Next update in 30 minutes.
