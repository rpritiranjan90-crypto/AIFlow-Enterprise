# AIFlow Enterprise v3.0 90-Day Public Launch Roadmap

## Overview
This roadmap details the 90-day operational, commercial, and growth strategy for scaling **AIFlow Enterprise v3.0** post-engineering certification.

---

## 1. Phase 1: Launch & Initial Acquisition (Weeks 1 - 2)
- **Week 1 (Public GA Launch)**:
  - Deploy Release Candidate `v3.0.0-GA` to primary production Kubernetes clusters.
  - Publish Product Hunt, Hacker News, and LinkedIn GA announcements.
  - Activate self-serve 14-day Pro trial on `app.aiflow.enterprise.io`.
- **Week 2 (Early Customer Onboarding)**:
  - Conduct daily customer success webinars and live onboarding sessions.
  - Monitor real-time telemetry via Grafana Executive & API dashboards.

---

## 2. Phase 2: Commercial Expansion (Month 1)
- **Growth & Marketing**: Launch targeted developer & enterprise engineering campaigns on LinkedIn, X, and Dev.to.
- **Enterprise Sales**: Initiate outbound outreach to Fortune 500 CTOs/CISOs focusing on SOC2/HIPAA compliance & self-hosted options.

---

## 3. Phase 3: Scaling & Retention (Months 2 - 3)
- **Month 2**: Expand customer success team and introduce automated health-score scoring in Customer Portal.
- **Month 3**: Conduct Quarterly Business Reviews (QBR) with initial enterprise accounts and plan v3.1 feature enhancements based on usage analytics.
