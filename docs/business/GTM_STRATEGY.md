# Go-To-Market (GTM) Commercial Strategy

## 1. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
- **Primary ICP**: Mid-Market & Enterprise Software Companies (100 - 5,000 employees).
- **Target Buyers**: CTOs, CISOs, Heads of AI/ML, VP of Engineering, Enterprise Architects.

## 2. Pricing Tiers
- **Free**: $0/mo (50k AI tokens, 10 workflows).
- **Starter**: $49/mo (500k AI tokens, 100 workflows).
- **Professional**: $199/mo (2.5M AI tokens, 500 workflows, 30+ connectors).
- **Business**: $499/mo (10M AI tokens, 2,000 workflows, SOC2 compliance).
- **Enterprise**: $1,499+/mo (Custom LLM training, dedicated Kubernetes clusters, 99.99% SLA).
